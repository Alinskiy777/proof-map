264	oxvp1cv	For a faceless art channel like this, a few things tend to matter more than usual: \- Timelapse the process instead of showing it real-time. "Drawing a \[specific subject\]" beats a vague title, because people click into process/art videos when they already know what they're about to watch get made. \- Music choice matters more than usual precisely because there's no talking - something that matches the pacing (calm for careful linework, upbeat for a fast timelapse) does a lot of the emotional work a voiceover would normally carry. \- Consistency in subject matter or style (always drawing a specific character type, or sticking to one medium) builds a recognizable channel identity faster than variety does at this size. None of this moves the numbers fast, but it keeps the videos watchable to people outside her existing circle, which is really the only lever that matters this early.
